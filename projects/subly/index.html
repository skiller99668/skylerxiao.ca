Subly

A sublease marketplace for students who move twice a year.

Student housing turns over every four months, and the listings live in a dozen group chats. Subly puts them in one place, on a map, with enough structure to actually compare them.

Auth, listing management, and location-based discovery. Supabase handles storage and access rules; the map layer does the searching.
