Waive

Turning intimidating government notices into plain language.

2nd place — STEMINATE Hacks 2026

Official letters are written for the people who send them, not the people who get them. Waive takes a government notice and returns two things: what it actually says, and what you can do about it next.

Built with three teammates over a weekend. I worked on the front end and on how the output is presented — the hard part was making the plain-language version feel trustworthy rather than like a guess.
